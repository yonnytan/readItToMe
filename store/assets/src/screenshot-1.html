Why listening wins when your eyes are tired

8 min read · Essays

Screens ask a lot of us. By the end of a long day the words are still there, but the will to decode them line by line has quietly gone.

The voices your computer already ships with are the same neural voices Siri uses. They are free, they are already downloaded, and most people never hear them.

Given a good voice, listening is not a lesser way to read. It is simply a different route into the same paragraph — one that leaves your eyes, and your posture, out of it.

What follows is a short case for reading with your ears, and a look at how far the free voices have come in the last three years.
