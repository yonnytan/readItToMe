Adoption held steady through the quarter, with the sharpest movement in the accessibility segment.

Participants who listened to long documents finished them 38% more often than participants who read the same documents on screen, and reported markedly less fatigue at the end of the session.

The effect held across age groups, and was strongest for documents longer than two thousand words.
