Read It To Me

Get the most human-sounding voices (free)

Read It To Me uses the voices already on your computer plus the ones Chrome ships with. Both are free. Downloading your system's premium voices makes a big difference — they are the same neural voices Siri and Windows Narrator use.

macOS

  1. Open System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content.
  2. Click the ⓘ next to System Voice, then Manage Voices…
  3. Download any voice labelled Premium or Enhanced (Ava, Zoe, Evan and Nathan are the most lifelike).
  4. Reopen this page and pick the new voice above.

Windows

  1. Open Settings → Time & language → Speech.
  2. Under Manage voices, choose Add voices and install your language pack.
  3. Voices named Natural are the neural ones — pick one above.

ChromeOS & Linux

  1. Chrome's own network voices (listed as Google …) are already available and sound natural.
  2. On ChromeOS you can add more under Settings → Accessibility → Text-to-Speech.

Keyboard shortcuts

Alt+R read or stop · Alt+P pause or resume · Alt+S stop. Change them at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

Privacy

Nothing you read is collected, stored or sent anywhere by this extension. Page text is passed straight to Chrome's speech engine, and your voice and speed settings sync through your own Chrome profile. Full policy: PRIVACY.md.
